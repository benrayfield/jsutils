Species-sized field · editable Stak

64-float operand stack per GPU invocation; function locals are separate. S(value) pushes, s() pops. This experiment retains the stack operations; the GPU driver may optimize them. Arbitrary edits can make a field unsuitable for distance-estimator ray marching.

Measure, don’t assume “2×”

Comparison uses the unmodified bundled example, your current camera, equal resolution and the same ray marcher. GPU timing excludes display/readback. GFLOP/s is a separate uniform-dependent arithmetic benchmark: multiply + add = 2 operations; no loads or transcendental functions counted. It is not a hardware instruction counter or a promise of fractal throughput. Missing/disjoint GPU timers never turn into CPU-based “GFLOP/s”.
