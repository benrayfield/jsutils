AeroForge 2D: does the flat plate fly?

One mathematically thin 6-foot line, not a blocky solid. A plate-mounted center actuator pushes existing air left and right just above the top surface. Viscosity and moving no-slip physics decide whether the sheet entrains the air above and creates net lift. The plate is free to fall, rise, drift, rotate, and tip over.
